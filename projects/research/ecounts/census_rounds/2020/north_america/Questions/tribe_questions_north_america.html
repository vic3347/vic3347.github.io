InstructionEspecifique a(s) etnia(s) indígena(s) em até dois registros.

Guatemala

Status
Yes
Year
2018
Form
Censo Nacional de Población y Vivienda 2017–2018 — Enumeration Form
Question
PCP12–PCP13
PCP12 asks how the person self-identifies according to origin or history; PCP13 asks which linguistic community the person belongs to when PCP12 = Maya.

Responses:

Code / itemResponse
PCP12Maya; Garífuna; Xinka; Afrodescendiente/Creole/Afromestizo; Ladina(o); Extranjera(o).
PCP13For persons identifying as Maya, record the Maya linguistic community; the form lists 22 Maya linguistic communities.

Question type: Subsumed under Indigenous/ethnolinguistic identity — there is no separate question using the word tribe
