Ethnicity Questions by Country

South America — countries documented in the supplied census questionnaires

No currently documented South American questionnaire in this initial set has been retained for this topic. Entries will be added only when the supplied questionnaire explicitly supports them.

Chile

Status
Yes
Year
2024
Form
Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024 — Cuestionario de Abordaje Aymara
Question
2.07
¿Cuáles etnias, pueblos o grupos indígenas podemos encontrar en esta aldea/comunidad?

Responses:

The questionnaire provides five write-in spaces for “Etnias, pueblos ou grupos indígenas” (ethnicities, peoples, or Indigenous groups).

Question type: Open-ended; community-level identification of ethnicities, peoples, or Indigenous groups

Brazil

Status
Yes
Year
2022
Form
Censo Demográfico 2022 — Questionário Amostra
Question
4.03
Qual a sua etnia, povo ou grupo indígena?

Responses:

For a person who declared or considers themselves Indigenous, the questionnaire asks for the Indigenous ethnicity/people/group and provides up to two records (4.03.1 and 4.03.2) using a selectable list.

Question type: Open/selectable-list response; conditional on Indigenous identification

Source: brazil_2022_long_enumeration_form_IPUMS_PT.pdf
