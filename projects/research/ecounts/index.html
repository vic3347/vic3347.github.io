This archive is part of the Global Enumeration Practices project. Forms were located through national statistical office websites, IPUMS International, the IHSN/World Bank catalog, and direct requests to statistical agencies. Register-based census systems are documented with their source registers and variable inventories in place of a traditional questionnaire.
