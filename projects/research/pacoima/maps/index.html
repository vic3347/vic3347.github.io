Change in African American Population, 1970–2023

This map visualizes changes in the African American population in Pacoima and surrounding areas from 1970 through 2023 using U.S. Census Data provided by Social Explorer.

Data sources include the U.S. Decennial Census, American Community Survey, NHGIS, and Social Explorer.
