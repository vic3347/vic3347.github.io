Profiles of Pacoima Census Tracts 1950-2024

The following links are profiles of Pacoima neghborhood. The 1950-1960 Census tracts are not harmonized to any other Census. The 1970-2010 Census tracts are all standardized to 2010 Census tracts and are directly comparable. The 2023 profile is based on American Community Survey Census Tract data and will vary some from previous years.

Year PDF File Description
1950 Pacoima_1950_Tract_Data.pdf 1950 Census tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries.
1960 Pacoima_1960_Tract_Data.pdf 1960 Census tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries.
1970 Pacoima_1970_Tract_Data.pdf 1970 Census tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries. 1970–2010 are harmonized to 2010 Census tract boundaries.
1980 Pacoima_1980_Tract_Data.pdf 1980 Census tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries. 1970–2010 are harmonized to 2010 Census tract boundaries.
1990 Pacoima_1990_Tract_Data.pdf 1990 Census tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries. 1970–2010 are harmonized to 2010 Census tract boundaries.
2000 Pacoima_2000_Tract_Data.pdf 2000 Census tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries. 1970–2010 are harmonized to 2010 Census tract boundaries.
2010 Pacoima_2010_Tract_Data.pdf 2010 Census tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries.
2023 Pacoima_2023_Tract_Data.pdf 2023 tract profiles of Pacoima neighborhood based on overlapping tracts with contemporary Pacoima neighborhood boundaries. Data comes from 5-year ACS.
