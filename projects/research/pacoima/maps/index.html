Change in African American Population, 1970–2023

This map visualizes changes in the African American population in Pacoima and surrounding areas from 1970 through 2023, highlighting long-term demographic shifts associated with migration, housing, and urban change.

Data sources include the U.S. Decennial Census, American Community Survey, NHGIS, and Social Explorer.
