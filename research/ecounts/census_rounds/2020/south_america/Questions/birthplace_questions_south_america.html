Birthplace Questions by Country

South America — countries documented in the supplied census questionnaires

Revised rule: Birthplace includes both direct questions about where a person was born and proxy birthplace questions that ask where the person's mother lived when the person was born.

Chile

Year
2024
Form
Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024 — Cuestionario Censal
Question
25
25. Cuando nació, ¿en qué comuna o país vivía su madre?

Responses:

Code / itemResponse
1En esta comuna — In this municipality
2En otra comuna — specify the municipality
3–9En otro país — Venezuela, Perú, Haití, Colombia, Bolivia, Argentina, or Otro país; specify the country

Interpretation: This is a proxy birthplace question: it asks where the person's mother lived when the person was born. Under the revised birthplace rule, it is included as a birthplace item.

Question type: 3 — proxy birthplace based on mother's place of residence at the person's birth
