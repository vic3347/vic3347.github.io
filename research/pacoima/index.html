Race & Inequality Over Time in Pacoima, CA

This project examines long-term patterns of racial composition, socioeconomic inequality, and spatial change in Pacoima, California, using historical census data, maps, and contextual neighborhood information.

Project Components

Historical Data

Decennial census data, demographic trends, and inequality indicators tracking race, age, poverty, and housing over time.

Economic Data

Historical data on businesses, economic conditions, and industry in Pacoima.

Maps

Spatial visualizations of demographic change, segregation, and neighborhood boundaries across decades.

Pacoima Information

Historical background, policy context, and local institutional factors shaping inequality in Pacoima.
