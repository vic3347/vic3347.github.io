Course development & curriculum

  • Responsible for internal and external department assessment and reviews as Chair of the Department.
  • Led the development of the Criminal Justice major and the restructuring of the Sociology major.
  • Developed the initial curriculum for the Bachelor of Social Work for approval by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education, and recruited the Program and Field Directors for the new BSW major.
  • Developed new minors in African American Studies and Latinx Studies.
  • Core member of the committee that developed the General Education Core at Rider University.
  • Developed and regularly taught courses in criminology, sociology, race and ethnicity, research methods, and social inequality at the undergraduate and graduate levels, including:
    • Introductory sociology & criminology
    • Research methods
    • Racism
    • Race and crime
    • Race, class and gender
    • Social demography
    • Internships & guided studies

Teaching roles & instructional leadership

Chair of Department of Sociology, Criminal Justice, and Social Work

Program Director for Internships in the Department of Sociology & Criminology at Rider University, overseeing placement, supervision, and assessment.

  • Internship Coordinator for Justice Studies at San José State University, graduate and undergraduate level.
  • Graduate instructor for Research Methods and Race & Crime at San José State University.
  • Senior Thesis and Honors Thesis Advisor at Stanford University for all juniors and seniors in African American Studies, Chicana and Chicano Studies, Asian American Studies, American Indian Studies, and Comparative Studies in Race and Ethnicity, guiding students through the thesis process with weekly writing workshops and scholarship support.

Advising, mentoring & high-impact learning

  • Advise students in African American Studies and Latinx Studies on course selection, minor requirements, and career planning.
  • Advised and mentored students through New Jersey’s Educational Opportunity Program (EOP), supporting first-generation and underserved students with academic, developmental, and scholarship guidance.
  • Honors College Advisor at Stanford University, supervising theses and research development alongside broader mentorship.
  • Represented Rider University at Bonner High Impact Institute meetings at Allegheny College (2013) and Siena College (2014).

Awards, grants & service supporting teaching
